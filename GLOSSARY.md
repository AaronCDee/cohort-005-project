# Course Platform

An online course platform where instructors publish courses and learners buy, watch and complete them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, in their own words, to have the money for a purchase sent back. Each one is Requested until someone on the team Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the learner when a Refund Request is Approved. A Declined request produces no Refund.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback (a dispute the cardholder raises with their bank, which this platform does not handle)

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase during which a learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a purchase can still get a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, not watched or completed past the limit, not a claimed team seat, and with no Refund Request already Requested.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer
